### SECTION 2: FOUR CORE GROUND OPERATIONAL PILLARS (2013–2024)
2.1 **Remedial Rural Education (Balangir & Puintala Blocks):**  
Providing free evening remedial tutoring in Mathematics, Science, and English for Class 6–10 students from economically challenged agrarian families to prevent high school dropouts. Over 350+ students actively supported across rural clusters.

2.2 **Women's Vocational Training & Tailoring School:**  
Empowering rural women, single mothers, and school dropouts with 6-month certified vocational stitching, garment construction, and pattern-making skills. Over 120+ women trained and equipped for dignified home-based or micro-enterprise self-reliance.

2.3 **Jal Seva — Heatwave Potable Drinking Water Relief:**  
Operating free, hygienic earthen pot water kiosks across critical transit nodes (bus stands, railway stations, government hospital perimeters) in Western Odisha during severe 45°C+ summer heatwaves. Over 10,000+ pedestrians, commuters, and daily-wage laborers served annually.

2.4 **Jan Ahar — Hospital Patient Attendant Nutrition:**  
Distributing hygienic subsidized or free meals to underprivileged patient attendants outside District Headquarters Hospital (DHH) Balangir, ensuring families facing medical distress do not go hungry.

### SECTION 3: 100% DIRECT DISBURSAL STANDARD & ZERO CASH POLICY
3.1 **Direct-to-College Tuition Remission:**  
All higher education scholarship subsidies are remitted directly to the verified institutional bank account of the accredited university, college, or polytechnic (payable to Bursar, Registrar, or Principal).

3.2 **Strict Zero Cash Policy:**  
The Trust strictly prohibits the disbursement or handling of physical currency. No cash, bearer cheques, or private bank account transfers are ever made to individual students, parents, relatives, or guardians under any circumstances.

3.3 **Zero Intermediaries & Anti-Commission Mandate:**  
The Trust employs zero educational agents, brokers, commission liaisons, or third-party fee collectors. Applications and appraisals are conducted directly with students and accredited colleges.

3.4 **100% Free Application Standard:**  
All scholarship applications, eligibility screenings, and counseling services provided by MECT are completely free of charge (₹0). The Trust never charges any application, prospectus, or processing fee.

### SECTION 4: EDUCATIONAL SCHEMES & SCHOLARSHIP PATHWAYS
4.1 **Mahavir Shiksha Protsahan Yojana:**  
- **Objective:** Direct tuition fee assistance for meritorious undergraduate and diploma students from economically challenged households in Odisha.
- **Coverage:** Accredited Bachelor's degree (BA, BSc, BCom, BTech, BCA, BBA) and State Council Polytechnic Diploma programs.
- **Mechanism:** Direct fee transfer to the college upon institutional admission or semester fee demand note verification.

4.2 **Pratibha Excellence Fellowship:**  
- **Objective:** Merit-based study stipend and competitive examination support for high-achieving graduates preparing for national and state competitive examinations (UPSC Civil Services, OPSC, GATE, UGC-NET, CAT).
- **Inclusions:** Academic mentorship, sponsored test series, and specialized study reference books.

4.3 **Project Samarth: Community Learning Centers:**  
- **Objective:** Grassroots community study hubs equipped with quiet reading halls, electricity, high-speed digital connectivity, and competitive reference libraries.
- **Phasing:** Pilot launch in Balangir and Khordha districts, followed by phased expansion.

### SECTION 5: ELIGIBILITY & SELECTION BENCHMARKS
5.1 **Domicile Requirement:**  
Applicants must be permanent bona fide residents of Odisha (verified through permanent residence certificate or documented schooling history in Odisha).

5.2 **Academic Merit Benchmark:**  
Candidates are expected to hold a minimum 60% aggregate in 10th, 12th, or diploma examinations (or equivalent CGPA). Special relaxation benchmarks may be designated by the Trustee Board for disadvantaged candidates.

5.3 **Economic Need Criteria:**  
Need-based priority is given to households with annual family income under ₹2,50,000/- per annum, verified through an official Income Certificate issued by a competent Revenue Authority (Tahasildar / Revenue Inspector).

5.4 **Blind Appraisal & Equity:**  
Candidate appraisals combine academic consistency and financial need evaluation conducted without demographic or geographic bias.
