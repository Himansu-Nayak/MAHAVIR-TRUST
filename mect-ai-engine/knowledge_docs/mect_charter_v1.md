### SECTION 1: NATURE AND PURPOSE OF THE TRUST
1.1 **Legal Entity & Scope:**  
Mahaveer Educational & Charitable Trust (MECT / MAHAVIR TRUST) is a public charitable educational trust initiative established in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India. Its foundational purpose is advancing higher education access, merit grants, academic mentorship, and community study facilities for deserving students across Odisha.

1.2 **Geographic Mandate:**  
The Trust operates with a comprehensive statewide mandate covering all 30 districts of Odisha:
- Coastal & Central Odisha: Khordha, Cuttack, Puri, Kendrapara, Jagatsinghpur, Jajpur, Nayagarh, Dhenkanal, Angul.
- Northern & Eastern Odisha: Balasore, Bhadrak, Mayurbhanj, Kendujhar.
- Western Odisha: Sambalpur, Sundargarh, Bargarh, Jharsuguda, Deogarh, Subarnapur, Balangir, Nuapada.
- Southern Odisha: Ganjam, Gajapati, Kandhamal, Boudh, Kalahandi, Rayagada, Nabarangpur, Koraput, Malkangiri.

### SECTION 2: 100% DIRECT DISBURSAL STANDARD & ZERO CASH POLICY
2.1 **Direct-to-College Tuition Remission:**  
All financial grants, tuition remissions, and scholarships awarded by the Trust are remitted directly to the verified institutional bank account of the accredited college, institute, or university (payable to the College Bursar, Registrar, or Principal).

2.2 **Strict Zero Cash Policy:**  
The Trust strictly prohibits the disbursement or handling of physical currency. No cash, bearer cheques, or private bank account transfers are ever made to individual students, parents, relatives, or guardians under any circumstances.

2.3 **Zero Intermediaries & Anti-Commission Mandate:**  
The Trust employs zero educational agents, brokers, commission liaisons, or third-party fee collectors. Applications and appraisals are conducted directly with students and accredited colleges.

2.4 **100% Free Application Standard:**  
All scholarship applications, eligibility screenings, and counseling services provided by MECT are completely free of charge (₹0). The Trust never charges any application, registration, prospectus, or processing fee.

### SECTION 3: PROPOSED EDUCATIONAL SCHEMES [DRAFT PROPOSALS]
3.1 **Mahavir Shiksha Protsahan Yojana:**  
- **Objective:** Direct tuition fee assistance for meritorious undergraduate and diploma students from economically challenged households in Odisha.
- **Coverage:** Accredited Bachelor's degree (BA, BSc, BCom, BTech, BCA, BBA) and State Council Polytechnic Diploma programs.
- **Mechanism:** Direct fee transfer to the college upon institutional admission or semester fee demand note verification.

3.2 **Pratibha Excellence Fellowship:**  
- **Objective:** Merit-based study stipend and competitive examination support for high-achieving graduates preparing for national and state competitive examinations (UPSC Civil Services, OPSC, GATE, UGC-NET, CAT).
- **Inclusions:** Academic mentorship, sponsored test series, and specialized study reference books.

3.3 **Project Samarth: Community Learning Centers:**  
- **Objective:** Establishing grassroots community study hubs in semi-urban and rural clusters equipped with quiet reading halls, electricity, high-speed digital connectivity, and competitive reference libraries.
- **Phasing:** Pilot launch planned for Khordha District, followed by phased expansion across other districts.

### SECTION 4: ELIGIBILITY & SELECTION BENCHMARKS
4.1 **Domicile Requirement:**  
Applicants must be permanent bona fide residents of Odisha (verified through permanent residence certificate or documented schooling history in Odisha).

4.2 **Academic Merit Benchmark:**  
Candidates are expected to hold a minimum 60% aggregate in 10th, 12th, or diploma examinations (or equivalent CGPA). Special relaxation benchmarks may be designated by the Trustee Board for disadvantaged candidates.

4.3 **Economic Need Criteria:**  
Need-based priority is given to households with annual family income under ₹2,50,000/- per annum, verified through an official Income Certificate issued by a competent Revenue Authority (Tahasildar / Revenue Inspector).

4.4 **Blind Appraisal & Equity:**  
Candidate appraisals combine academic consistency and financial need evaluation conducted without demographic or geographic bias.

### SECTION 5: STATUTORY DISCLOSURES & REGULATORY NOTICES
5.1 **Section 80G and 12A Tax Exemption Disclosure:**  
The Trust is established under the Indian Trusts Act framework. Formal Section 80G and Section 12A registration credentials and certificates will be publicly notified on https://mect.org.in/ upon formal issuance by the Income Tax Department. The Trust does not issue premature 80G tax receipts prior to official statutory certification.

5.2 **Independent Financial Audits:**  
All trust expenditures, grant transfers, and receipts are subject to mandatory annual statutory audit by independent Chartered Accountants, and are maintained in the administrative secretariat.
